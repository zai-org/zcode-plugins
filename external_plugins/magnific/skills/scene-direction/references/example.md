# Worked example - "The Logbook", Block 2 of 4

An original example for calibrating density and length.
About 1,300 words for 30 seconds is typical.

**Brief from the director:**
Mara, a lighthouse keeper in her sixties, refuses to give the station logbook to Jonas, a young company courier sent to collect it during a storm.
She is calm and kind; he is polite, then pushes.
Lines, exact: Jonas "They just need the book, Mrs. Holm." / Mara "They can come for it themselves." / Jonas "I came for it." / Mara "You came in out of the rain."
End on Jonas leaving without the book, Mara alone.
Handheld but gentle; three-quarter angles; nothing straight-on.

**Prompt:**

```
SCENE
Block 2 of 4. Thirty seconds, 2.39:1 (21:9). The kitchen of a lighthouse keeper's cottage at night, a storm outside.
Jonas, a company courier, has come for the station logbook; Mara, the keeper, will not give it up.
Arc: courtesy, a polite push, a quiet refusal that ends the conversation. Naturalistic drama, cold storm light against warm stove light.

REFERENCES
@char_mara_video_v1 - VIDEO ELEMENT - MARA. Face, grey braid, hands, knitted cardigan and voice match the reference.
Use the voice heard in the reference for every line: low, unhurried, a slight northern accent.
@char_jonas_video_v1 - VIDEO ELEMENT - JONAS. Face, wet dark hair, company rain jacket and voice match the reference. Use his reference voice for every line.
@loc_kitchen_stove_v1 - THE STOVE SIDE. Layout, materials and practical light: iron stove at frame-right with an orange glow, a kettle, a wooden table with the logbook on it.
@loc_kitchen_door_v1 - THE DOOR END OF THE KITCHEN. The door, a coat hook and a small rain-streaked window.
Both location references control look and layout only; their camera angles and framing are not inherited.
@prop_logbook_v1 - THE LOGBOOK. A thick cloth-bound ledger with a cracked green spine. It keeps this exact appearance in every shot.

SPACE
The table runs left to right across the room. Mara sits at the stove end (frame-right in the master), the stove behind her.
Jonas stands at the door end, the door behind him, about two metres from the table.
The logbook lies on the table at Mara's right hand. The axis runs from the door to the stove; the camera stays on the window side of it.

OPENING FRAME
Medium three-quarter shot from the window side at seated eye height. Mara is already seated with her right hand resting flat on the logbook.
Jonas is already standing just inside the door, rain dripping from his jacket, hood down. The kettle is already steaming.

FORMAT
Multi-shot sequence, 30 seconds, six shots, hard cuts at 5.0, 10.5, 15.0, 21.0 and 25.5 s.

OPTICS
Medium shots: 47° diagonal field of view. Close-ups: 29°. The final wide: 63°. Lens locked within each shot.

CAMERA
Handheld at rest: a slow breathing drift, never a jolt. Each shot holds one position; at most one slow push per shot.
The camera never crosses the axis.

SHOTS
SHOT 1 · 0.0-5.0 s · MEDIUM - JONAS
Camera: window side, 1.6 m from Jonas, at his chest height, three-quarters to his left.
Starts: Jonas inside the door, rainwater running off his sleeves onto the floorboards.
Action: he wipes his palms down the front of his jacket, then holds both hands slightly open toward the table.
Line: JONAS: "They just need the book, Mrs. Holm." - offered lightly, as if it were a small favour.
Sound: rain on the window, the kettle's low hiss, water dripping on wood.
Ends: Jonas waiting, hands still open. Cut on his last word.

SHOT 2 · 5.0-10.5 s · CLOSE - MARA
Camera: window side, 1.2 m from Mara, at her eye height, three-quarters to her right; the stove glow on the right side of her face.
Starts: Mara seated, right hand flat on the logbook.
Action: she looks at his wet sleeves before she looks at his face. Her thumb moves once along the cracked spine.
Line: MARA: "They can come for it themselves." - plainly, the way one gives directions.
Ends: her eyes on his. Cut as she finishes.

SHOT 3 · 10.5-15.0 s · MEDIUM CLOSE - JONAS
Camera: window side, at his eye height, a slow push from medium to medium close over the full shot.
Starts: Jonas where he stood, hands lowering.
Action: he takes one step toward the table and stops at its edge; his right hand rests on the back of the empty chair.
Line: JONAS: "I came for it." - quieter than before, testing her.
Ends: Jonas at the table edge, right hand on the chair back. Cut on the end of the line.

SHOT 4 · 15.0-21.0 s · CLOSE - MARA, INSERT OF THE HAND
Camera: low, at table height beside the logbook, looking up past her right hand to her face; 29° field of view.
Starts: Mara's right hand flat on the book.
Action: she slides the logbook two hands' widths toward herself, then folds both hands on top of it.
She holds his look for a full second, then the corner of her mouth softens.
Line: MARA: "You came in out of the rain." - gently, almost an invitation to sit.
Sound: cloth spine sliding on wood; the kettle starting to whistle.
Ends: both hands folded on the logbook. Cut as the whistle rises.

SHOT 5 · 21.0-25.5 s · MEDIUM - JONAS
Camera: window side at his chest height, static.
Starts: Jonas at the table edge.
Action: he lets go of the chair, looks at the logbook once more, nods once, pulls his hood up and turns to the door. He opens it; wind pushes rain across the threshold.
Sound: the door latch, a gust, louder rain.
Ends: the door closing behind him. Cut on the latch.

SHOT 6 · 25.5-30.0 s · WIDE - THE KITCHEN
Camera: the stove-side corner, waist height, 63° field of view, static.
Starts: Mara alone at the table, hands on the logbook, the closed door in the background.
Action: she lifts the kettle off the stove with her left hand and the whistle dies. Rain streaks the small window.
Ends: Mara pouring one cup. Hold to the end.

PERFORMANCE - MARA
The work is in her eyes and hands; she is fully occupied with this task.
THE EVENT (shared, never spoken): keeping this a polite conversation between two decent people.
WANTS FROM JONAS: to make him go back without the book and without feeling he failed.
BECAUSE: the book is forty years of her own handwriting, and she likes the boy.
IN THE WAY: he has orders, and her own anger at the company is close to the surface.
DOING: keeping her hand on the book as if it were simply where her hand rests.
BEATS:
- Before her first line - reads him; eyes go to his wet sleeves first, then his face.
- "They can come for it themselves." - redirects him; eyes hold his, unhurried.
- Sliding the book toward herself - closes the subject, without hurry.
- "You came in out of the rain." - releases him; eyes soften, offering him a way to leave with dignity.
LIVING EYES: gaze always engaged in the task, natural blinking, the eyes move when the thought moves.

PERFORMANCE - JONAS
The work is in his eyes and hands; he is fully occupied with this task.
THE EVENT (shared, never spoken): keeping this a polite conversation between two decent people.
WANTS FROM MARA: to get her to hand over the book so he does not have to take it.
BECAUSE: his job depends on it, and he would hate himself for grabbing it.
IN THE WAY: her calm, which leaves him nothing to push against.
DOING: dripping by the door, then one step to the table, his hand on a chair he is not invited to use.
BEATS:
- "They just need the book, Mrs. Holm." - makes it small; eyes check whether she will make this easy.
- "I came for it." - tests her; eyes on her hand on the book, then back to her face.
- Her last line - takes it in; eyes drop to the book once, and he decides to let it go.
LIVING EYES: gaze always engaged in the task, natural blinking, the eyes move when the thought moves.

PHYSICS
Rainwater runs off Jonas's jacket and darkens the floorboards around his boots. Steam rises from the kettle spout and bends in the draught when the door opens.
The cloth logbook slides with friction, not glide. Wind moves the coat on the hook when the door opens.

LIGHT
Two sources only: the stove's warm orange glow from frame-right, and cold blue storm light from the small window.
Both faces keep readable eyes; Mara warmer, Jonas colder until he steps toward the stove.

AUDIO
English only. Both characters speak their lines exactly as written, each in the voice of their reference.
Closed sound list: rain on glass and roof, wind gusts, the kettle hiss rising to a whistle, dripping water, floorboard creaks, the cloth spine on wood, the door latch, one cup being filled.
The soundtrack is dialogue and these sounds only.

LOOK
Naturalistic drama, fine film grain, soft highlight roll-off, warm tungsten against cold blue, deep but readable shadows.

QUALITY
Readable faces and hands on held moments, natural motion blur on the door and the gust, stable anatomy and continuity.

LOCKS
Exactly two people: Mara at the stove end, Jonas at the door end. Mara stays seated for the whole scene.
The logbook stays on the table under Mara's hands; Jonas's hands never reach it.
The door is behind Jonas and the stove behind Mara in every shot.
The scene ends with Mara alone, pouring one cup.
```
