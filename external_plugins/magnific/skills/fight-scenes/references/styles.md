# Fighter styles

Pick one style per fighter.
Contrast between styles is what makes a fight readable: the audience understands who is skilled by comparison.
Each style lists how it reads on screen, typical moves, and a lock line to add to the FIGHT LOCK.

| Style | Reads on screen as | Typical moves | Lock line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Economical interceptor** | Calm, almost motionless, then sudden straight lines; wins in two to four actions by hitting first | ST1, FW1, FW2, PU1, IN1, IN2, KI1 | "He hits along the shortest path, kicks low, and waits motionless between bursts." |
| **Boxer** | Square guard, bobbing head, combinations, no kicks | ST2, PU1-PU5, IN4 | "He fights only with his fists, head moving, combinations of two or three." |
| **Clinch fighter** | Grabs the head, knees and elbows at close range, shins to the legs and body | CL1, EL1, EL2, KI2, KI4 | "He closes the distance, controls the head and strikes with knees, elbows and shins." |
| **Grappler** | Absorbs hits to get hold, then throws and pins | CL2, CL3, CL4, FA1 | "He takes hits to get hold, then throws and pins." |
| **Centre-line chain puncher** | Narrow, square, walks straight forward with fast short punches, sticks to the opponent's arms | PU8, PU9, IN2 | "He stays square and narrow, walking forward behind fast straight punches down the centre." |
| **Brawler** | Big wide swings, telegraphed, angry, tires fast | ST5, wide hooks and haymakers, headbutts | "He swings wide and heavy, every punch loaded from the shoulder." |
| **Trained guard or soldier** | Disciplined, coordinated in groups, uses batons, holds and weapons | WP2, WP4, CL1 | "They move as a trained unit but each reacts at his own moment." |
| **Creature** | Explosive leaps, wide clawing swings, roars, superhuman force, unpredictable timing | Leaps, claw swings, throws | "Its swings are wide and fast, its strength throws a man across the room." |

## Mixing styles in one scene

- **Hero versus thugs:** an economical interceptor against brawlers. The hero looks precise because they look wild.
- **Equal duel:** two disciplined styles (for example an interceptor against a clinch fighter) so each style has answers and the fight can trade.
- **Clash of schools:** two fighters of different disciplined styles, each with its own lock line, in the same FIGHT LOCK.

## The defaults to override

When nothing is specified, video models tend to produce:
- wide windmill swings and high spinning kicks;
- opponents queuing to attack one at a time without reason;
- hits that stop in front of the target;
- falls that start before contact;
- every opponent moving in sync.

Each style's moves and the writing laws exist to replace these defaults with specific, positive evidence.
