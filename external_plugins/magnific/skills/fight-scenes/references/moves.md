# Move library

Each entry gives a code, the planning name, and ready prompt phrasing written as geometry plus reaction.
Adapt left and right, names, targets and shot size.
Codes are for planning conversations ("intercept with T2, finish with K3"); they never go into the prompt.

## ST - Stances and stillness

**ST1. Bladed stance (economical striker)**
`He stands turned almost sideways to the other man, his right shoulder and right foot toward him, feet a little wider than his shoulders, knees soft, the back heel lifted slightly so he rests on the balls of his feet. His front hand floats loosely at the height of his own shoulder, the rear fist rests against his jaw, elbows tucked against his ribs, chin down, his face calm.`

**ST2. Square guard (boxer)**
`He faces the other man almost square, feet staggered, weight even, both fists high beside his cheeks and elbows covering his ribs, chin tucked behind his front shoulder, bobbing slightly at the knees.`

**ST3. Open-hand calm (hidden readiness)**
`He holds both palms up at chest height as if to calm things down, but his feet have already shifted into a staggered stance and his weight sits on the balls of his feet.`

**ST4. Stillness**
`He stops completely in his bladed stance: feet planted, hands still at guard, breathing slow, only his eyes following the other man. The stillness lasts a full second.`

**ST5. Wild stance (brawler)**
`He faces square and leans forward, fists low at his waist, shoulders rolling, rocking from foot to foot.`

## FW - Footwork

**FW1. Short slide in**
`He slides forward half a step, front foot first and back foot following the same distance, his stance and guard unchanged.`

**FW2. Explosive entry**
`He covers more than a metre in one burst, his front foot reaching and his back foot driving, arriving already striking.`

**FW3. Angle off the line**
`He steps diagonally forward and to his left, out of the path of the attack, so the other man's strike passes where he stood.`

**FW4. Circling**
`He circles to his right in small sliding steps, staying out of reach of the other man's right hand.`

**FW5. Retreat and return**
`His front foot snaps back to where his back foot was, the attack falls short, and the foot shoots straight back out.`

## PU - Punches and hand strikes

**PU1. Front-hand straight**
`His front fist travels a short straight line from where it floated at guard into the other man's mouth, his body weight sliding in behind it. The head snaps straight back and the man's heels lift.`

**PU2. Rear-hand straight**
`His back fist drives straight from his jaw to the point of the other man's chin as his back hip turns forward. The man's head jerks back and his knees sag.`

**PU3. Hook**
`His front arm swings in a tight horizontal curve, elbow at shoulder height, the knuckles landing on the side of the other man's jaw. The head turns sharply away and the body follows it sideways.`

**PU4. Rising strike under the chin**
`His fist rises from waist height straight up under the point of the other man's chin, his legs pushing up into it. The jaw slams shut, the head tips back and the man lifts onto his toes.`

**PU5. Body shot**
`His fist sinks into the soft side below the other man's ribs. The man folds toward the fist, his elbow dropping to cover too late, and he bends forward.`

**PU6. Back-of-the-fist strike** (three anchors)
`Like a backhand slap made with a fist, his front arm whips out sideways and the back of his knuckles cracks against the other man's temple. The head snaps sideways in the direction of the swing.`

**PU7. Hammer strike** (three anchors)
`Like hammering a nail, he brings the bottom of his clenched fist straight down onto the back of the bent man's neck. The man drops flat onto his chest.`

**PU8. Chain punches**
`His fists fire one after the other straight down the centre line, five or six short punches in two seconds, each landing on the face as he walks forward. The man's head jolts back with each one and he stumbles backward.`

**PU9. Palm heel**
`The heel of his open palm drives up into the other man's nose, fingers pointing to the ceiling. The head snaps back and the eyes water.`

## EL - Elbows and knees

**EL1. Horizontal elbow**
`At arm's length too close for a punch, his elbow swings flat across, the point of it landing on the other man's cheekbone. The face turns hard away and he staggers sideways.`

**EL2. Knee to the body**
`He pulls the other man's head down with both hands and drives his knee straight up into his stomach. The man's breath bursts out and his feet leave the ground for a moment.`

**EL3. Back elbow**
`Grabbed from behind, he drives his elbow straight back into the other man's ribs. The man folds around it and his grip opens.`

## KI - Kicks

**KI1. Stop kick to the knee**
`As the other man steps in, the sole of his front foot shoots out sideways and lands flat against the front of the man's knee. The leg buckles backward and the man drops toward the floor.`

**KI2. Low kick to the thigh**
`His back shin swings in a low curve into the outside of the other man's thigh with a heavy slap. The leg buckles and the man limps sideways.`

**KI3. Push kick** (three anchors)
`Like kicking a door shut, he drives the flat sole of his foot straight into the other man's stomach. The man is launched backward off his feet into [the bar / the wall / the table].`

**KI4. Shin kick to the body**
`His back shin swings up into the other man's ribs at waist height. The man folds sideways around it.`

**KI5. Stomp**
`Like stamping on a box to flatten it, he drives his heel straight down onto the fallen man's thigh.`

## IN - Interceptions and parries

**IN1. Stop hit**
`The other man's shoulder starts to turn for a big right swing, and in that same instant his front fist is already landing on the man's chin. The swing never finishes, and the man staggers back two steps.`

**IN2. Slap aside and hit**
`The other man's punch comes straight in; his rear palm slaps it aside at the wrist while his front fist lands on the man's nose in the same movement.`

**IN3. Pull and strike**
`He grabs the other man's wrist and yanks it down and toward himself, and the man's face falls straight into his rising fist.`

**IN4. Duck under**
`The swing passes over his head as he drops at the knees, and he rises inside the other man's arm already striking.`

**IN5. Forearm deflection**
`His forearm meets the incoming punch and knocks it off its line, and his other fist lands three short shots into the man's body.`

## CL - Clinch, control and throws

**CL1. Head control**
`He clasps both hands behind the other man's head, pulls it down to his chest and holds it there.`

**CL2. Hip throw**
`He turns his back into the other man's belly, pulls the man's arm over his shoulder and bends forward. The man rolls over his hip and slams flat onto his back.`

**CL3. Trip**
`He hooks his foot behind the other man's heel and shoves his chest. The man's leg is swept out and he falls backward.`

**CL4. Break a grab from behind**
`Grabbed from behind, he drops his weight, steps to the side and drives his elbow back, turning out of the arms.`

**CL5. Rear head hold**
`Standing behind him, he wraps one arm under the other man's chin and clamps his other hand on the back of his head.`

## WP - Weapons

**WP1. Knife thrust, angle off and disarm**
`The knife thrusts straight at his stomach; he steps diagonally off the line, both his hands close on the knife wrist, and he bends it outward until the knife drops from the man's fingers.`

**WP2. Stick or baton swing, close the distance**
`The baton swings down at his head; he steps in under the swing so it passes behind him, his forearm meets the man's arm at the elbow, and his other fist lands on the man's jaw.`

**WP3. Gun at close range**
`The gun points at his chest from arm's length; his hand slaps the gun off his body line and grips the barrel, twisting it toward the man's thumb until his fingers open.`

**WP4. Electric baton**
`A short black baton; when switched on, a small, contained blue-white crackle runs between the two metal points at its tip and lights the man's knuckles.`

## FA - Falls and finishing states

**FA1. Straight back**
`He falls straight backward like a cut tree, his body stiff, and lands flat with his arms out.`

**FA2. Face down**
`His knees fold and he drops forward onto his face, his arms limp at his sides.`

**FA3. Crumple**
`His legs give way and he folds down into a heap where he stood.`

**FA4. Into an obstacle**
`He flies backward into [the table], which breaks under him, and he slides off onto the floor among the pieces.`

**FA5. Dazed on the ground**
`He lies on his side, eyes unfocused, one hand slowly pawing at the floor, trying and failing to push himself up.`
