# Impact physics, reactions and sound

What makes a hit land on screen is what happens after the contact.
Write each layer that the shot size can show.

## Layers of a convincing impact

1. **Contact point:** the flesh or cloth deforms around the fist, foot or elbow for an instant.
2. **Chain through the body:** the struck part moves first and the rest follows like a whip: the head, then the shoulders, then the hips, then the feet.
3. **Loose matter:** sweat, spit, hair and loose clothing keep moving after the body stops, and hang in the air during slow motion.
4. **Weight and feet:** the feet react last: a stagger, a heel lifting, a knee buckling.
5. **Environment:** what the body hits moves, breaks or rings: glasses on the bar, a lamp, a door.
6. **Camera:** in handheld coverage, the camera jolts on contact and pushes toward it.
7. **Sound:** the specific sound of the contact (below), and a beat of quieter room sound after a big hit.
8. **Persistence:** marks, blood, torn clothing and broken objects stay for the rest of the scene.

## Reaction by direction of the strike

| Strike direction | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Straight to the face | The head snaps straight back, heels lift, a step or two backward |
| Rising under the chin | The jaw slams shut, the head tips back, the body lifts onto the toes |
| Horizontal to the jaw | The head turns hard away from the strike, the body follows sideways |
| Downward onto the back or neck | The body drops flat toward the floor |
| Into the body | The torso folds toward the strike, breath bursts out, the hands drop to cover |
| To the front of the knee | The leg buckles backward, the body pitches forward |
| To the outside of the thigh | The leg buckles sideways, a limp |
| Push to the stomach or chest | The whole body flies backward, feet leave the floor |

## Force levels

Use at most one heavy finisher per shot so it stands out.

| Level | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Light | A sting: the head flicks, the eyes blink, the fighter keeps going |
| Heavy | Real damage: a stagger, a fold, a knee touching down |
| Finisher | The fight ends: a fall, an unfocused stare, the body limp |

## Delayed reactions

Some hits are more convincing with a delay: a body shot to the side under the ribs lands, the man holds for half a second, then folds and drops to his knees.
Write the delay explicitly.

## Falls

| Fall | Phrasing |
|---|---|
| Straight back | Stiff, like a cut tree, arms out |
| Face down | Knees fold, he drops forward, arms limp |
| Crumple | Legs give way, he collapses where he stood |
| Into an obstacle | He flies into it, it breaks or rings, he slides down |

## Sound list for the AUDIO block

Choose the ones in play and list them, because unlisted hits tend to be silent:

- fist on face: a dull, wet smack
- fist on body: a deep thud and a burst of breath
- shin on thigh: a heavy slap
- elbow on bone: a sharp crack
- foot on chest: a hollow thump
- body on floor: a heavy thud and the slap of hands
- body through a table: splintering wood or bursting glass, then shards settling
- cloth: jacket leather creaking, fabric tearing
- breath: grunts, sharp exhales, ragged breathing after the fight
- weapons: a knife clattering on tile, a baton's electric crackle, a gun's dry click when empty
