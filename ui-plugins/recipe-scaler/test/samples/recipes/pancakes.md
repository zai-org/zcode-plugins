# Fluffy Pancakes

Serves 4

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tspn baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Method

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl whisk the milk, eggs and melted butter, then pour into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
3. Rest the batter for 5 minutes, then cook 1/4 cup at a time on a hot buttered pan, about 2 minutes per side.
